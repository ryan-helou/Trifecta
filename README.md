# Trifecta

**A triathlon training app that builds you a day-by-day plan for your race and adjusts it when life gets in the way.**

Triathletes train for three sports (swimming, cycling and running) plus strength work, and planning months of training is hard. Trifecta asks about your race, your date, your experience and your best times, then lays out every session from now until race day, with targets for each one. You can add it to your phone's home screen like an app, and an iPhone version is also in the works. It was built for its owner and a group of training friends.

## What you can do with it

- Pick a race (Olympic, half Ironman, full Ironman or a custom distance) and get a full plan that builds up, peaks and tapers before race day.
- Open any session to see what to do: warm-up, main set, cool-down, and the pace, power or heart-rate to aim for.
- Mark sessions done, skip or move them, swap the sport, or ask it to ease off this week or rebuild the plan from today.
- Connect Strava (or Apple Health on iPhone) so finished workouts are matched to your plan automatically.
- Track your progress: weekly training time, how much of the plan you completed, and time spent per sport.
- Follow friends' training in a shared feed and league, and optionally chat with an AI coach.
