A NEW LOOK — Trifecta has a new mark and app icon, and it now follows your phone's light or dark setting properly, launch screen included.

ASK THE COACH TO CHANGE SOMETHING — "Give me an easier one today" now produces an easier session, not advice about one. The coach shows you exactly what it will do and waits for you to confirm; the plan itself is still changed by the same engine the buttons use.

SHARE A SESSION — Finish a session and you can share it as a card. It reports what you actually did, not what was prescribed.

THE PLAN EASES ITSELF — Mark a session rough and the next few days come down straight away, instead of waiting a fortnight for a trend to notice.

FIXES — Swiping down on a session sheet dismisses it instead of selecting text. Sessions you have already finished no longer show under "Up next". Weeks are labelled plainly rather than restarting the count inside each phase. A synced activity can no longer be counted four times. The keyboard no longer covers the box you are typing in, and the status bar no longer sits on top of the screen underneath it. Setting a race date now looks tappable, and the last race option is no longer cut off.
