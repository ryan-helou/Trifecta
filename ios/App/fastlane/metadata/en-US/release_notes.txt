LEAGUES — Start a points competition with friends. Log swims, rides, runs and gym sessions, and watch the leaderboard move through the week.

APPLE HEALTH, HANDS OFF — Workouts now sync automatically when you open the app, and a session already recorded elsewhere is matched rather than counted twice.

PROGRESS LOOKS FORWARD — The Form chart projects the arc all the way to race day, so you can see where the plan is taking you, not just where you have been.

ONBOARDING — Preview your plan and adjust it before creating an account. New to triathlon? There is a plain-language explainer for picking a race.

CLEARER PLAN — Training weeks are labelled simply "Week 1", "Week 2", and so on, instead of coaching jargon.

FIXES — The Profile tab could render blank in some cases; that is fixed. Signing out now properly ends your session. The day-one "missed sessions" warning is gone, swiping a session is easier to discover, and eased sessions say so.
